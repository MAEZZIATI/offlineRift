# Historical studies and cleanup

Superseded dated reports, the old fourteen-page synthesis and its specialized renderer,
and ADC-only selection/profile utilities were archived at:

`data/archive/six-policy-migration-20261109/`

`HISTORY.json` records original paths, archive paths and SHA-256 digests. The archive
also snapshots the previous README, documentation and dependency declaration. No raw
matches, datasets, model weights or failed/interrupted experiment reports were deleted.
Existing experiment/source snapshots remain under their original data directories.

Before the next robustness study, the completed six-policy synthesis was copied into
`data/archive/coherent-robust-20261109/`, with its own SHA-256 `manifest.json`.

The maintained product now consists of one macro coach and five role specialists.
Historical ADC results do not imply that the product is ADC-only, and historical
training successes do not establish strategic usefulness. Current documentation and
synthesis report the six-policy architecture and its offline evidence.
